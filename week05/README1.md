The reverse-engineered diagram contains six tables, including two junction tables, Enrolment (Student-Course) and CourseAllocation (Course-Lecturer), which are now real tables with foreign keys. It also shows data types and constraints such as NOT NULL, UNIQUE and DEFAULT that my Week 4 diagram did not include. My Week 4 model used a table called Teaching for the Course-Lecturer link; the reference schema replaces it with CourseAllocation, which also records the academic session.
